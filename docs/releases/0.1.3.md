Fixes.

- The settings window follows your desktop's light or dark preference, read
  from the XDG portal when the compositor reports no theme (#20, thanks
  @salemsayed, who fixed it in Rune).
- On GNOME, KDE Plasma and quickshell, whose shells keep the notifications
  name themselves, Hokum says plainly which shell holds it and how to turn its
  notifications off, instead of a bare error (#10, thanks @AMluisXVI and
  @Redouane64).
- Hokum exits with status 78 when the desktop can't run it, and
  `hokum.service` no longer restarts it in a loop (#10).
- On compositors without layer-shell (GNOME), Hokum says so and exits at
  startup (#10).
- Card shadows are no longer clipped on their inner side, away from the screen edge (#3, thanks
  @uncomplexity).
